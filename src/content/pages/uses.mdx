## Editor

- **Helix** for almost everything. Built-in LSP and motion grammar good enough that I never went back to Neovim.
- A handful of personal language-server configs in `~/.config/helix/`.
- **JetBrains Rider** when I need the inspector, which is once a quarter.

## Hardware

- A 14-inch Lenovo P14s running Linux Mint. Replaced a Framework I lost on a train.
- A Filco Majestouch 2, MX brown, dragged through nine countries and four houses.
- Sennheiser HD 600 → Topping E30/L30 stack for working music.

## Dotfiles

- `github.com/gme/dotfiles` — fish, helix, kitty, sway.
- The `kitty.conf` is the only file in there that has survived since 2019 unchanged.

## Reading

I read on a Kobo Libra, sideloaded from Calibre. Books on the [reading page](/reading).
